# Retail Sales Analytics: Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

An interactive data analytics and visualization project designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and discount strategies. The dashboard helps identify key business opportunities for increasing sales and improving profitability.

# Short Description / Purpose

The Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to provide a clear view of overall sales and profit  performance.

The project combines Excel, MySQL, and Power BI to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize retail sales data. It enables businesses to identify high-performing products, regions, customers, and segments while evaluating the relationship between discounts, sales, and profitability.

# Tech Stack

The project was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – Main platform used to create the interactive dashboard and data visualizations.
📂 Microsoft Excel – Used for initial data cleaning, transformation, and feature creation.
🗄️ MySQL – Used for data storage, SQL-based analysis, aggregation, and business queries.
🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures and KPIs such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Average Order Value, and Profit Margin.
🔄 Power Query – Used for data transformation and preparation within Power BI.
📐 Data Modeling – Used to organize the dataset and establish the required structure for analysis.
📁 File Formats – .xlsx/.csv for data, .sql for SQL analysis, and .pbix for the Power BI dashboard.

# Data Source

Dataset:  AI-generated synthetic retail sales dataset created using ChatGPT and intentionally designed with data-quality issues for cleaning and analysis practice.
The project uses a retail sales dataset containing transactional information about orders, customers, products, regions, categories, segments, quantities, pricing, discounts, sales, and profit.


# Features / Highlights

• Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transactional data, but raw sales data can make it difficult to quickly understand what is driving revenue and profitability.

Key questions such as: Which regions generate the most sales? Which products and categories are most profitable? Which customers contribute the most revenue? How do discounts affect profit? Which customer segments perform best? … are difficult to answer efficiently from raw data.
To deliver an interactive visual tool that:

Enables users to monitor overall retail sales and profitability.
Identifies high-performing products, regions, customers, and segments.
Analyzes sales trends over time.
Evaluates the relationship between discounts and profit.
Supports data-driven decisions to improve sales and profitability.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

Key KPIs (Top Section)- Displays Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value, and Profit Margin % to provide a quick overview of business performance.
Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart)- Shows sales performance across different months, helping identify sales trends and high- or low-performing periods.
Sales by Region (Bar Chart)- Compares sales across regions to identify the strongest-performing markets and areas that may require additional sales or marketing efforts.
Profit by Category (Column Chart)- Displays profit generated by each product category, helping identify which categories contribute most to overall profitability.
Top 5 Products (Column Chart)- Highlights the best-performing products based on sales and profit, supporting decisions related to inventory, promotions, and product strategy.
Sales by Customer Segment (Donut Chart)- Compares sales across customer segments such as Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office to understand which customer groups contribute most to revenue.
Discount vs Profit (Scatter Chart)- Analyzes the relationship between discount levels, sales, and profit to understand whether higher discounts are contributing to sales without negatively affecting profitability.
Top 10 Customers (Bar Chart)- Ranks customers based on sales, helping identify high-value customers and opportunities for targeted retention and marketing strategies.
Sales by Order Size (Donut Chart)- Shows the contribution of different order sizes to total sales and helps understand the overall order mix.
Interactive Filters / Slicers- Users can filter the dashboard by Year, Month, Region, Category, and Segment to explore specific areas of the business.

# Business Impact & Insights

Sales Optimization: Identify high-performing regions, products, and customer segments to focus sales and marketing efforts.

Profitability Improvement: Analyze profit margins and discount patterns to develop more effective pricing and discount strategies.

Product Strategy: Identify top-performing products and categories to support inventory planning and promotional decisions.

Customer Strategy: Identify high-value customers and customer segments for targeted marketing and retention strategies.

Regional Analysis: Compare regional performance to determine where additional resources and sales efforts could generate better results.

Data-Driven Decision Making: Transform raw retail transaction data into actionable insights that help the business make informed decisions and ultimately improve sales and profitability.
